How to play

Link words through compounds in exactly four steps.
  1. You are given a start word and a target word.
  2. Each step, enter a new word that forms a compound or common phrase with the previous word (e.g. FIRE + FLY = firefly).
  3. You are told the length of each intermediate word.
  4. Reach the target in exactly four steps.
  5. Normal and hard mode keep separate streaks. Hard mode disables hints and undo.
