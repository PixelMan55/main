How to play

Rearrange letters to solve the waffle.
  1. The grid holds six interlocking 5-letter words — three across and three down.
  2. All the correct letters are already present; they are just scrambled.
  3. Tap two tiles to swap them. Each swap costs one move.
  4. Green = correct letter in the correct spot (locked).
  5. Yellow = letter belongs in that word but wrong position.
  6. Gray = letter does not belong in either intersecting word.
  7. You have 15 swaps. Solve in 10 or fewer for five stars.
