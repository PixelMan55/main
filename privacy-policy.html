Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ladder (the "Game"), made by PixelMan Games ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

WhatWhen it's collected
Anonymous account identifierAutomatically, when you first open the Game, so your progress can be saved and synced
Gameplay data (puzzle progress, guesses, completion times, streaks, hard-mode status, settings such as dark mode/sound)As you play, so your stats and preferences persist between visits
Display name (optional)If you choose to appear on a leaderboard
Custom ladder data (start/target words, generated code)If you create a custom ladder to share
Email addressOnly if you voluntarily subscribe to the weekly puzzle email
Locally stored data (offline progress)Stored in your browser so you can keep playing without a connection

We do not ask for your name, physical address, or payment information to play the Game.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and sync your puzzle progress, streaks, and statistics across sessions and devices;
  • To let you create and share custom ladders via a code or link;
  • To display leaderboard entries you choose to participate in;
  • To send the weekly puzzle email, if you subscribe;
  • To keep the Game working reliably and to diagnose technical issues.

3. How Information Is Stored

Gameplay data is stored using our cloud database provider (Firebase) under an anonymous account tied to your device/browser, unless you sign in through an available account option. If the Game can't reach our servers, it falls back to storing your progress locally in your browser so you can keep playing; that local copy is not visible to us until it syncs.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who host our data and infrastructure (e.g. our cloud database and hosting provider), solely to operate the Game;
  • Other players, limited to what you choose to make visible — such as a leaderboard display name, or a custom ladder you actively share via its code/link;
  • Authorities, if required to comply with a legal obligation.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The Game uses browser local storage (and similar technologies) to remember your settings and to keep an offline copy of your progress. This is used to make the Game function, not for advertising or third-party tracking.

6. Your Choices

  • You can play without ever entering a display name or email address.
  • You can unsubscribe from the weekly puzzle email at any time using the link in those emails.
  • You can clear your browser's local storage to remove locally saved progress from your device.
  • You may request that we delete data associated with your account by contacting us (see below); note that this may reset your streaks and statistics.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information such as an email address from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Data Retention

We retain gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so your streaks and statistics remain intact. You may request deletion at any time as described above.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the information we store, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Users

The Game may be accessed from outside the country where our servers and service providers are located. By using the Game, you understand that your information may be processed in other countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to make a data request, contact us via pixelman.net.
